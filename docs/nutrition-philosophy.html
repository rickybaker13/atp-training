🥗 Nutrition Philosophy

Our Commitment to Youth Athlete Nutrition

Our Core Belief

ATP is designed with the health and well-being of young athletes as our top priority. Our nutrition guidance follows evidence-based principles developed specifically for growing athletes.

🚫 NO Caloric Restriction for Youth Athletes

ATP will NEVER recommend low-calorie diets, weight loss plans, or caloric restriction for middle school or high school athletes.

Why? Because:

  • Growing bodies require adequate energy for physical development, brain function, and athletic performance
  • Caloric restriction during adolescence can lead to serious health consequences including hormonal disruption, bone density issues, and disordered eating patterns
  • Young athletes naturally need MORE calories than their non-athletic peers
  • Energy deficiency impairs athletic performance, increases injury risk, and slows recovery

Core Principles

1. Addition, Not Restriction

Our nutrition plans focus on ADDING nutritious foods to an athlete's diet, not taking foods away. We believe in teaching young athletes what foods fuel their bodies best, building positive relationships with food, and creating sustainable eating habits that last a lifetime.

2. Age-Appropriate Guidance

Our AI coach tailors every nutrition plan based on the athlete's age group, with specific guidelines for middle school (ages 11-14) and high school (ages 14-18) athletes.

3. Body Composition vs. Weight Loss

When young athletes want to "improve body composition," our approach focuses on food QUALITY, not quantity. We emphasize proper fueling around training, adequate protein for muscle development, and nutrient timing for performance — NEVER caloric restriction.

Middle School Athletes (Ages 11-14)

  • Higher caloric calculations to support growth spurts
  • Lower protein targets (0.5-0.7g per lb) than adult athletes
  • Emphasis on calcium, vitamin D, and iron for development
  • Kid-friendly, approachable meal options
  • No supplements — whole foods only
  • Guidance designed for parents/guardians who prepare meals

High School Athletes (Ages 14-18)

  • Still prioritizing growth and development
  • Moderate protein targets (0.6-0.8g per lb)
  • School-schedule-friendly options
  • Portable snacks for busy student-athletes
  • No extreme diets or "clean eating" rigidity
  • Emphasis on healthy relationship with food

What We Will Never Do

  • Never recommend caloric deficits for athletes under 18
  • Never suggest restrictive diets (keto, very low carb, etc.) for youth
  • Never recommend diet pills, fat burners, or weight loss supplements
  • Never promote weight-focused goals for growing athletes
  • Never create meal plans that could trigger disordered eating behaviors

For Parents & Guardians

As a parent of a young athlete, you play a crucial role in their nutrition. Our plans include:

  • Guidance for meal preparation
  • Quick, practical options for busy families
  • School lunch and snack ideas
  • Pre/post-practice nutrition tips
  • Age-appropriate portion guidance

If you have concerns about your child's weight or eating habits, we strongly encourage consulting with a pediatric sports dietitian or your child's healthcare provider.

Scientific Foundation

Our nutrition guidelines are based on:

  • American Academy of Pediatrics recommendations
  • International Olympic Committee consensus on youth athlete nutrition
  • National Athletic Trainers' Association position statements
  • Current sports nutrition research for adolescent athletes
Remember: Proper fueling = Better performance + Healthy development
