Frequently Asked Questions

How do I get my personalized training plan?
Tap "Training Plan" on the home screen and answer a few questions about your sport, position, goals, and experience. Our AI coach will generate a custom program just for you.
Can I change my training plan later?
Yes! You can purchase a Plan Regeneration ($1.99) at any time to get a fresh plan with new inputs or updated goals.
How do teams and leaderboards work?
Create a team or join an existing one using a team code. Complete workouts to earn points, and compete with teammates on daily, weekly, and all-time leaderboards.
Is the nutrition plan safe for young athletes?
Absolutely! Our nutrition plans are designed specifically for each age group. We never recommend caloric restriction for middle school or high school athletes. Read our Nutrition Philosophy
How do I request a refund?
All purchases are processed through Apple's App Store. To request a refund, visit reportaproblem.apple.com or contact Apple Support directly.
Can I use ATP on multiple devices?
Your purchases are linked to your Apple ID and can be restored on any device signed into the same account. Your progress syncs across devices when connected to the internet.
How do I delete my account and data?
Email us at atptraining@proton.me with the subject "Data Deletion Request" and we'll remove your data within 30 days.

Response Time

We typically respond to support requests within 24-48 hours. For urgent issues, please include "URGENT" in your email subject line.
