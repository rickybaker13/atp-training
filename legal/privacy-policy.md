# Privacy Policy

**ATP: Ascent Training Protocol**

*Last Updated: January 29, 2026*

---

## Introduction

ATP: Ascent Training Protocol ("ATP," "we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

## Information We Collect

### Information You Provide
- **Account Information:** Email address (optional, for team features)
- **Profile Information:** Age, height, weight, sport, position, training experience
- **Training Data:** Workout completions, points earned, progress data
- **Team Information:** Team name, team membership (if you join a team)

### Information Collected Automatically
- **Usage Data:** App interactions, features used, workout completions
- **Device Information:** Device type, operating system version
- **Analytics:** Anonymized usage patterns to improve the app

## How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:
- Generate personalized AI training and nutrition plans
- Track your workout progress and points
- Enable team features and leaderboards
- Improve our app and services
- Provide customer support

## Data Sharing

We do **NOT** sell your personal information. We may share data with:
- **AI Service Providers:** To generate your personalized plans (data is not stored by these providers)
- **Team Members:** Your display name and points are visible to teammates on leaderboards
- **Service Providers:** Analytics and infrastructure services that help us operate the app

## Data Storage and Security

- Your data is stored securely using industry-standard encryption
- Training plans and progress are stored locally on your device and in our secure cloud database
- We retain your data only as long as necessary to provide our services

## Your Rights

You have the right to:
- Access your personal data
- Request deletion of your data
- Opt out of team features
- Export your workout data

To exercise these rights, contact us at atptraining@proton.me

## Children's Privacy

ATP is designed for athletes of all ages, including minors. We are committed to:
- Collecting only information necessary for app functionality
- Never displaying personal information publicly (only display names on leaderboards)
- Providing age-appropriate content and recommendations
- Parents/guardians may contact us to review or delete their child's data

## In-App Purchases

When you make a purchase:
- Payment processing is handled entirely by Apple's App Store
- We do not collect or store payment information
- Purchase history is associated with your Apple ID, not our servers

## Third-Party Services

Our app uses:
- **OpenRouter AI:** For generating training and nutrition plans
- **Supabase:** For secure data storage and team features
- **Expo/React Native:** For app infrastructure

Each service has its own privacy policy governing their data practices.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy in the app and updating the "Last Updated" date.
