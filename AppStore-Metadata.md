## App Description (4000 characters max)

Unlock your potential with ATP: Ascent Training Protocol – your personal AI-powered training coach.

Whether you're preparing for your next season, leveling up your skills, or competing with teammates, ATP delivers customized training programs built specifically for you using advanced artificial intelligence.

**PERSONALIZED AI TRAINING PLANS**
Answer a few questions about your sport, position, experience level, and goals. Our AI coach analyzes your inputs and generates a complete training program tailored to your needs – not a generic template, but a protocol built just for you.

**MULTI-SPORT SUPPORT**
ATP supports athletes across all major sports:
• Baseball & Softball
• Basketball
• Football
• Soccer
• Track & Field
• Tennis
• Swimming
• Volleyball
• And more

**OPTIONAL NUTRITION GUIDANCE**
Take your performance to the next level with AI-generated nutrition plans. Get personalized meal recommendations and macro targets based on your training goals, dietary preferences, and schedule.

**COMPETE WITH YOUR TEAM**
Create or join teams and compete on leaderboards. Earn points for completing workouts and hitting nutrition goals. See how you stack up against teammates with daily, weekly, and all-time rankings.

**TRACK YOUR PROGRESS**
Log completed workouts and watch your points grow. ATP keeps you motivated with achievement tracking and progress visualization.

**SHARE YOUR WINS**
Celebrate your accomplishments by sharing completed workouts, point milestones, and leaderboard rankings directly to social media.

**KEY FEATURES**
